OUR STORY

Five Years of Gratitude

For five years, Aseda Praise has brought thousands together in Tarkwa for an Easter experience of worship, thanksgiving and community.

"Not just an event, but an altar of thanksgiving raised for the entire region."

We believe that access to powerful, life-changing worship should not have a price tag. That is why Aseda Praise remains 100% free and open to everyone, drawing people from all walks of life into the presence of God every Easter.
