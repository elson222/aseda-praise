5-Year Milestone (2021 – 2026)

Half a Decade of Praise, Gratitude & Charity

Over the past 5 years, Aseda Praise has stood as a beacon of spiritual renewal and social responsibility across Tarkwa and the Western Region. What began as a passionate gathering of thanksgiving has transformed into a major community movement uniting gospel legends, youth, churches, and civic leaders.

Every edition combines profound spiritual worship with practical Christian love—delivering medical assistance to the elderly, supporting healthcare facilities, and hosting thousands under one banner of praise.

Apinto Government Hospital Outreach

5-Year Charitable Health Screening for the Aged

As part of Aseda Praise's 5-Year Anniversary milestone, the planning committee in partnership with health professionals at **Apinto Government Hospital, Tarkwa** conducted a major free medical screening drive dedicated to senior citizens and elderly community members.

  • Free Hypertension, Blood Pressure & Diabetes Checks
  • Comprehensive Eye Examinations & Prescribed Glasses Support
  • Free Prescription Medications & Nutritional Welfare Packages
  • One-on-One Consultations with Apinto Hospital Doctors
