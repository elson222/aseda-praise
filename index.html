About the Event

WHERE GHANA
WORSHIPS

Aseda Praise is an annual, non-denominational praise and worship night hosted in Tarkwa, Ghana. Every year, thousands of believers from across the region and beyond converge to lift one voice in gratitude, faith, and corporate worship.

The event features anointed ministers of music, electrifying worship moments, and a powerful demonstration of the Spirit — all completely free and open to everyone.

“Ten thousand hearts, one voice, one spirit — that’s what Aseda Praise is.”
