Our Sacred Vision

Giving Thanks & Serving Tarkwa

Aseda Praise is Western Region's premier annual gospel gathering in Tarkwa, Ghana. Born out of a deep heart of thanksgiving to God, it brings together tens of thousands of worshippers, ministers, and gospel icons under one roof.

Alongside atmosphere-shifting worship, Aseda Praise actively serves the community through major medical outreach and hospital welfare projects in Tarkwa.

Official Community Project

Dedication of Tarkwa Apinto Children's Ward

Aseda Praise puts faith into action! Through our charitable outreach, the Aseda Praise team officially renovated and dedicated the Tarkwa Apinto Government Hospital Children's Ward, providing modern healthcare facilities, beds, and medical support for sick children and families in Tarkwa.

  • Renovation & Dedication of Children's Medical Ward
  • Donation of Medical Equipment & Patient Supplies
  • Annual Free Health Screening for Senior Citizens
