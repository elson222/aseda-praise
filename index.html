Our Story

Five Years of Gratitude

What started as a simple gathering of gratitude has grown into the Western Region's most anticipated spiritual event. For five incredible years, Aseda Praise has brought together thousands to lift one unified voice in worship.


We believe that access to powerful, life-changing worship should not have a price tag. That is why Aseda Praise remains 100% free and open to everyone, drawing people from all walks of life into the presence of God.

"Not just an event, but an altar of thanksgiving raised for the entire region."

Founders & Lead Sponsors

Driven by a shared vision to impact their community through faith and action, our founders established Aseda Praise as a beacon of hope and gratitude in the Western Region.

Their dedication goes beyond the stage, ensuring that the event remains accessible to all while actively contributing to local healthcare and community development.

"Aseda Praise was never about a concert. It was always about saying thank you."
